About

I work where creativity, music and technology meet.

My background is in telecommunications engineering, with a master’s degree in Deep Learning for Multimedia Processing and studies in classical music. My work brings together two sides of my world: the technical and practical, and the creative and artistic.

I am particularly interested in what happens when these different views come together. My research in AI and music comes from that curiosity about bringing technology and artistic practice side by side.

I am a PhD candidate at the Department of Musicology at the University of Oslo, Norway. There I investigate the use of AI in musical creativity and explore new ways of combining computational techniques with artistic practice.

Research interests

  • Music technology
  • Machine learning
  • Computational creativity
  • Artificial intelligence and music
  • Generative AI and music creation
  • Human–AI collaboration
  • Music information retrieval
  • Computer music
